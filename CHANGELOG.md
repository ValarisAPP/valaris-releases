# Changelog

The main changes of every Valaris release. Full release notes, downloads and SHA-256 checksums are on the
[Releases](https://github.com/ValarisAPP/valaris-releases/releases) page.

## 1.6.2 — in progress
- **Valaris Hub in the app.** A new «Valaris Hub» tab in the «✨ Skill» window: 30 ready-made skills made and
  checked by our team (resumes, business plans, SQL, homework, stories, trips and more), in 9 languages. Without
  Pro you can keep up to 3 of them; Valaris Pro gives all. «Add to Valaris» on
  [valaris.app/hub](https://valaris.app/hub) opens the web version on that skill, «in the Windows app» opens the
  installed Valaris (a `valaris://` link; an already open window shows the skill).
- Links to the website pages from the Windows app (Terms, Privacy, Valaris Hub) open valaris.app.

## 1.6.1 — 28 October 2026
- **AI voice:** answers can be read aloud by a soft neural voice, six voices to choose from.
- **Agent keys:** keyboard shortcuts for agent mode, listed in the in-app help.
- **Task price:** after an agent task Valaris shows what it cost on OpenRouter.
- Website: «Made with Valaris» portfolio of projects built by the agent, with time, price and the task.

## 1.6.0 — 25 October 2026
- **Add to an answer while it is being written:** type while the model answers and press Enter.
- **Hide to the tray:** ✕ can hide Valaris next to the clock instead of closing it.
- OpenRouter shows Valaris correctly in your activity; the installer is checked file by file during the build.
- Website: new [Security and privacy](https://valaris.app/security.html) page.
